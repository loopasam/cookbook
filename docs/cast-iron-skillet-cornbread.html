Cast-Iron Skillet Cornbread

Servings: 8–10

Ingredients

  • 🌽 1¼ US cups coarse cornmeal (about 200g; not cornflour)
  • 🌾 95g plain flour (¾ US cup)
  • 🍬 50g sugar (¼ US cup)
  • 🧂 1 tsp kosher salt (or ½ tsp fine salt)
  • 🥄 2 tsp baking powder
  • 🫙 ½ tsp baking soda
  • 🥛 80ml whole milk
  • 🐄 240ml buttermilk
  • 🥚 2 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 🧈 113g unsalted butter, melted; reserve 14g for the skillet

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 220°C conventional / 200°C fan with a 23cm cast-iron skillet inside.
  2. Whisk cornmeal 🌽, plain flour 🌾, sugar 🍬, salt 🧂, baking powder 🥄 and baking soda 🫙 together.
  3. Whisk in whole milk 🥛, buttermilk 🐄 and eggs 🥚, then melted butter 🧈, keeping the reserved 14g aside. Mix just until combined.
  4. Carefully remove the hot skillet; reduce oven to 190°C conventional / 170°C fan. Swirl reserved butter 🧈 over the base and sides, then immediately pour in batter.
  5. Bake on the centre rack for 20–25 min, until the centre is firm and a skewer comes out clean. Rest 10–15 min before serving warm.

Notes

  • Butter shortfall: use 85g butter + 23g neutral oil; reserve 14g of the butter for the skillet and mix the rest with the oil into the batter.
  • Metric weights are approximate conversions; cornmeal weight varies by grind.
  • Adapted from Alex Guarnaschelli’s Cast Iron Skillet Corn Bread, Food Network.
