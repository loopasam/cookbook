Lemon Poppy Seed Muffins

Servings: 12 muffins

Ingredients

  • 🌾 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 🍚 1 cup sugar
  • 🌽 1/3 cup cornmeal
  • 🌱 3 tbsp poppy seeds
  • 🥄 2 tsp baking powder and 1/2 tsp salt
  • 🥣 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 🥚 2 eggs
  • 🧈 113g butter, melted and cooled
  • 🍋 1/4 cup bottled lemon juice and zest of 1 lime
  • 🍯 Glaze: 1/4 cup sugar, 3 tbsp bottled lemon juice, 1 tbsp water

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 200°C. Line a 12-cup muffin tin
  2. Whisk flour 🌾, sugar 🍚, cornmeal 🌽, poppy seeds 🌱, baking powder, and salt 🥄
  3. Whisk Greek yogurt 🥣, eggs 🥚, butter 🧈, lemon juice, and lime zest 🍋
  4. Fold wet into dry just until combined. Divide between cups
  5. Bake 18–20 min, until a toothpick comes out clean
  6. Heat glaze 🍯 until dissolved; brush over the warm muffins
