Crispy Baked Fish With Tartar Sauce

Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 🐟 4 thick cod or other white fish fillets, about 170g each, skin on or off
  • 🥄 240ml mayonnaise (1 US cup)
  • 🫒 5 tbsp capers, drained and coarsely chopped
  • 🥒 4 tbsp finely chopped pickles
  • 🌿 2 tbsp finely chopped tarragon, dill or parsley
  • 🍶 1 tsp Worcestershire or soy sauce
  • 🧄 1 small garlic clove, finely grated
  • 🍋 1 large lemon, zest + 1 tbsp juice; remainder in 4 wedges
  • 🍞 ½ US cup panko breadcrumbs (120ml)
  • 🫗 2 tbsp olive oil, plus extra for greasing
  • 🧂 Salt and black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 200°C conventional, rack in upper part. Mix mayonnaise 🥄, capers 🫒, pickles 🥒, herbs 🌿, Worcestershire sauce 🍶, garlic 🧄 and lemon zest 🍋. Season tartar sauce with salt and plenty of black pepper 🧂.
  2. Separately mix panko breadcrumbs 🍞 with olive oil 🫗; season with salt and pepper 🧂.
  3. Pat fish fillets 🐟 dry, season lightly and place on a lightly greased or foil-lined baking tray. Spread just under 1 tbsp tartar sauce per fillet; press about 2 tbsp panko mixture on each. Reserve remaining sauce for serving.
  4. Bake until nearly cooked, 10–15 min for 1.3–2cm-thick fish fillets 🐟 (check thinner ones earlier); centre about 52–54°C before grilling.
  5. Meanwhile, stir 1 tbsp lemon juice 🍋 into the reserved tartar sauce. Keep it separate from utensils that touched raw fish.
  6. Switch to grill; cook on upper rack 2–3 min, watching closely, until crumbs are golden and fish fillets 🐟 flake easily. Aim for 63°C in the centre. Serve with reserved tartar sauce, lemon wedges 🍋 and black pepper 🧂.

Notes

  • Adapted from NYT Cooking. Original final fish temperature: 60°C; 63°C used here for standard food safety.
