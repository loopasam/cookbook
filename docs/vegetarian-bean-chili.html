The Best Vegetarian Bean Chili

Servings: 6–8

Ingredients

  • 🫘 2 × 400g cans chickpeas, liquid reserved
  • 🍅 800g canned whole tomatoes, with juice
  • 🌶️ 240ml chile paste, made with water rather than chicken stock
  • 🥫 2 chipotles in adobo, finely chopped, plus 2 tbsp adobo sauce
  • 🫗 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 🧅 1 large onion, finely diced
  • 🧄 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 🟤 2 tbsp ground cumin (inferred; quantity missing in source photo)
  • 🌿 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 🍶 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 🫙 1 tsp Marmite
  • 🟥 2 × 400g cans red kidney beans, drained, liquid reserved
  • 🥃 2 tbsp vodka, bourbon, tequila or Cognac
  • 🧂 Salt, to taste
  • 🌽 2–3 tbsp instant cornmeal, such as Maseca

Steps

  1. Drain chickpeas 🫘, reserving liquid; pulse roughly in a food processor (about 3 × 1 second). Mix chickpea liquid with tomatoes and juice 🍅, crushing into roughly 1cm chunks; stir in chile paste 🌶️, chipotles and adobo sauce 🥫.
  2. Heat vegetable oil 🫗 in a heavy pot over medium-high. Soften onion 🧅 without browning, about 4 min. Stir in garlic 🧄, ground cumin 🟤 and dried oregano 🌿 for 30 sec; add soy sauce 🍶 and Marmite 🫙 and stir another 30 sec.
  3. Add tomato mixture, chopped chickpeas 🫘 and red kidney beans 🟥. Add reserved kidney-bean liquid to barely cover beans. Bring to a boil, then gently simmer about 1½ hours, stirring occasionally, until thick and rich. Add more bean liquid if too thick or sticking.
  4. Stir in vodka or alternative spirit 🥃; season with salt 🧂. Gradually whisk in instant cornmeal 🌽 until the desired thickness is reached.
  5. Serve, or cool promptly, refrigerate overnight for better flavour, then reheat. Top as desired and serve with corn chips or warm tortillas.

Notes

  • Toppings: grated cheddar, sour cream, chopped onion or scallions, sliced jalapeños, diced avocado and/or chopped coriander.
  • Cumin quantity inferred with Sam's approval, not verified from the printed recipe.
  • The source references a separate chile-paste recipe (p. 259), not supplied. Use a prepared paste of suitable heat; 240ml of a concentrated hot-chilli paste may be far too hot, so start with less and adjust.
