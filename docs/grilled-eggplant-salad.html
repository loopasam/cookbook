Grilled Eggplant Salad

Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 🍆 1 large eggplant
  • 🍅 1 plum tomato, diced
  • 🍋 1½ tsp red wine vinegar
  • 🧂 ½ tsp kosher salt, more to taste
  • 🌿 ½ tsp chopped fresh oregano
  • 🧄 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 🫗 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 🌿 3 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 🌑 Black pepper, to taste
  • 🫒 Capers, for garnish (optional)
  • 🫓 Grilled pita bread, for serving

Steps

  1. Heat grill to medium-high. Prick the eggplant 🍆 all over with a fork and place on the grill. Close the cover and cook, turning occasionally, until very soft and skin is blistered, about 15 minutes
  2. When cool enough to handle, scoop out the insides of the eggplant 🍆 and coarsely chop
  3. Transfer to a bowl and toss with tomato 🍅, vinegar 🍋, salt 🧂, oregano 🌿, and garlic 🧄
  4. Stir in olive oil 🫗 and parsley 🌿. Season with pepper 🌑 and more salt if needed
  5. Garnish with capers 🫒 if desired. Serve with warm pita bread 🫓

Notes

  • Let the grilled eggplant drain in a colander while cooling — the bitter juices escape leaving a milder flavour
  • For more smoky char, slice into ¾" slabs, brush with olive oil, and grill both sides instead of cooking whole
  • Lemon juice works well in place of red wine vinegar
  • Make it a few hours ahead to let the garlic mellow
