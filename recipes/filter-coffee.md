---
title: Filter Coffee
category: drinks
servings: 1-3 big mugs
---

## Ingredients
- ☕ Ground filter coffee
- 💧 Water

## Steps
1. For 1 big mug: use 3 scoops coffee and fill water to the **4–6** line
2. For 2 big mugs: use 6 scoops coffee and fill water to about the **8–12** equivalent
3. For 3 big mugs: use 9 scoops coffee and fill water to about the **12–18** equivalent
4. For a bigger pot for 2–3 people: use **7–8 scoops** and fill around the **12-ish** line
5. For Sam, err weak: start with **7 scoops** rather than 8–9

## Notes
- Scale linearly from the 1-mug baseline.
- If it tastes too thin, increase by 1 scoop next time.
